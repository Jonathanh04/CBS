# CBS

## AI Controversies Trainer

`ai-controversies-trainer/index.html` is a self-contained study app built from the summary of the lecture *Controversies of Artificial Intelligence* and Exercise Class 3 (AI and regulation), with Mügge (2024) and Liebig et al. (2024).

Open the file in any browser. It has three modes:

- **Quiz**: 60+ multiple-choice questions with explanations, filterable by topic, with a review of missed questions and a "retry missed" round.
- **Flashcards**: 30 key concepts. Mark cards as known; progress is saved in your browser.
- **Drills**: put-in-order exercises (controversy mapping, cold→hot scale, governance timeline, market-creation logic) and an EU AI Act risk-tier sorter.
