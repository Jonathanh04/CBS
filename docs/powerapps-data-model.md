# Partnership Desk — SharePoint data model (Power Apps version)

**v1.0 · 2026-10-12.** The Power Apps app stores all data in **SharePoint lists** in the CBS Talks
Team/SharePoint site. This is the Power Apps form of the schema in
[`lean-spec.md`](lean-spec.md) §7. The rules (Fit, Access, quadrant, flags, work queue) are
calculated in the app, never stored.

Create the lists with [`powerapps/sharepoint-lists-setup.xlsx`](../powerapps/sharepoint-lists-setup.xlsx)
(SharePoint → New → List → From Excel). It is generated by `tools/build_sharepoint_setup.py`.

## Conventions

- **Every list's key is SharePoint's built-in `Title` column:** the company name in Companies,
  and the contact name in Contacts. Other lists link to a company by putting the
  **company name in `Title`**, or in the `Company` column for Contacts. Keys are plain text, so the
  setup stays simple and the app does the matching. **Company and contact names must be unique**,
  and the app warns about duplicates.
- **Column names have no spaces,** so SharePoint's internal names equal the display names that the
  formulas use. You can change a column's display name later, but **never its internal name**.
- **Allowed values are enforced by the app's dropdowns.** The SharePoint columns are plain
  "Single line of text", which avoids fiddly Choice-column setup and makes changes easy.
- **Yes/No values are stored as the text `Yes` / `No`.**
- **Dates are "Date and time" columns (date only); numbers are "Number" columns.**

## How the app uses the lists

- **Loading:** at start, the app loads every list into a collection. The lists are small (well under
  2,000 rows, Power Apps' standard limit for this kind of load), so this avoids SharePoint
  delegation problems.
- **Calculations:** Fit, Access, quadrant, last contact, flags and the work queue are calculated
  from the collections in the app, following lean-spec §3.
- **Saving:** every edit is written to SharePoint, then the collections are refreshed.
- **Permissions:** everyone in the CBS Talks Team can read and edit. Version history in SharePoint
  shows who changed what.

## Ownership and handover

- The lists live in a **Team-owned** SharePoint site with **at least two board members as owners**.
- The Power Apps app has the Partnerships Manager as owner and **at least one co-owner**. Before
  a student's CBS account is closed, ownership must be transferred (handover checklist).
