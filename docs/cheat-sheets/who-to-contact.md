# Cheat sheet — Who to contact

*One page. Pick the role by partnership type and company size, not by seniority.*
Part of the [lean specification](../lean-spec.md).

## 1. Three rules first

1. **Warm path beats the right title.** If anyone at the company knows us, ask them to introduce us to the right role.
2. **Match seniority to the ask.** Small asks (< 10k DKK, non-cash) → manager level. Larger asks → head of function, who brings in their director.
   **Never write to the CEO first** unless the company has < 50 employees, the CEO *is* the speaker, or we already know them.
3. **Use the official route if there is one.** If the company has a sponsorship application form, use it, and mention it if you also contact someone.

## 2. Role by partnership type and company size

| Partnership type | Small (< 50 in DK) | Mid-size (50–999) | Large (1,000+) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Recruitment** | Founder / first HR hire | Talent Acquisition Manager | Campus Recruiter / University Relations / Early Careers |
| **Event / recurring sponsor** | Founder | HR or Marketing Manager (whoever owns employer branding) | Head of Employer Branding, or Partnerships/Sponsorship Manager |
| **Annual partner** | (rare) | HR or Marketing Director, via our existing contact | Head of Employer Branding (owner), introduced to their director by them |
| **Speaker** | Founder directly | The person directly if we have a path; otherwise Communications | Communications / the executive's assistant; directly only with a warm path |
| **Knowledge** | Founder | The expert (e.g. head of research / chief economist) | The expert, with Communications informed |
| **Content** | Founder | Marketing / Communications Manager | Communications or Employer Branding |
| **Networking** | Founder / partner | Community or Partnerships Manager | Community / Ecosystem Manager |
| **In-kind (venue, catering)** | Owner / manager | Events or Sales Manager | Events / Sales Manager; the venue manager approves |

## 3. Titles to search for (English / Danish)

| Role | Search terms |
|---|---|
| Campus / early careers | Campus Recruiter, Early Careers, Graduate Recruitment, University Relations / Studenterrekruttering |
| Talent acquisition | Talent Acquisition, Recruitment Manager / Rekrutteringschef |
| Employer branding | Employer Branding, Talent Attraction / Employer Branding-ansvarlig |
| Marketing | Marketing Manager, Brand Manager / Marketingchef |
| Partnerships | Partnerships Manager, Sponsorship / Sponsoransvarlig |
| Communications | Head of Communications / Kommunikationschef |
| Events | Events Manager, Sales & Events / Eventchef |
| CSR | CSR Manager, Sustainability / Bæredygtighedschef |

Where to look: LinkedIn (filter by "Copenhagen Business School" to find alumni), the company's team page, career fair contact lists, people who have posted about student partnerships.

## 4. Record it properly

- Store the contact with **source** and **date verified**. Re-check that they're still in the role if it was over 6 months ago.
- Only work email addresses that are **published or given to us**, or LinkedIn. **Don't guess email addresses.**
- In the first conversation, ask: *"Who else would usually be involved in a decision like this?"* and update the record.
